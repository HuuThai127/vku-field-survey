1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

Facility maintenance and student auditing teams at VKU frequently conduct asset reviews in campus basements, lecture theaters, and lab suites with absent or intermittent internet connectivity. VKU Field Survey solves this through an offline-first architecture with cache-first app shell delivery, local IndexedDB draft auto-saving, sequential offline synchronization queueing, and an installable native Android APK wrapper.
